


there is an interesting ritual in Tibetan culture/religion where they meticulously construct a complex painting right at the center of their building. for months, they spend enormous amounts of effort and time completing this complex and delicate work. when it's done... they simply... dismantle it, removing and cleaning away the colored powder completely.

it removes the symbolic appearance from that beauty at the center of the building and returns its value to its core: simply powder. powder that simply gets removed, with zero thought given to the "wasted" time, effort, or money.

i love this concept.
