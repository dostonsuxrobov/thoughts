

how to become strong? 
how to become invincible? one asked.

wise person answered: 
work more. ask less.
when you say, "i need you," you are weak. when you say, "i don't need you," you are strong.
when you waste, you become easy to leverage. money saved is money earned, and it is not only about money.
do not cling to anything, not even life itself. you will lose everything anyway.
think of yourself as already dead. take what is left and live properly. be useful. show respect, love, and help others.
you do not need much. in fact, you need nothing in the first place, because you never truly owned anything, and you never truly will.
