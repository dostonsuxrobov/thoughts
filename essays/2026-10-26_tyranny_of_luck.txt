

luck sounds like a good event but at the core it hides core value of the mechanism surrounding us. everything is a mechanism, life itself is a giant mechanism, we exaggerate them simply because our brain capacity cannot possibly understand all the variables all at once.
luck depends on uncontrollable set of variables while mechanism is reliable and controllable, with one you need to pray with another you can act upon. i believe the strongest version of mechanism is not effort to understand all of it but rather use it to your own advantage, something like compounding is a powerful mechanism that can punch through the uncontrollable variables very reliably.
lets look at the examples : weight loss, one can definitely be born with bad genetics which keeps fat at all costs while another can be born with perfect genetics which gains muscles and burns fat effortlessly, so does it mean they both permanently destined based on their own genes ? NO, the body mechanism is absurdly simple when it comes to weight management, calories in > out = gain, calories in < out = loss, lucky events of sudden diet or sudden inflow of "bad food" rarely help while compounding effect of consistent effort for prolonged timeframe almost guarantees result.
when Bill Gates founded Microsoft he grew arrogant very fast and started going too aggressive on monopolistic behaviour which actually makes sense, when you get such massive success so early by doing something you would do anyways like playing a game, you get enormous inflow of ego validation which gradually grows into God complex very fast.
when Fyodor Emelianenko slipped inside the Octagon and fell down, his opponent battered him with punches again, again . . . . and again, looking at it was horrible, single unlucky moment was about to determine his career or potentially his health, but he took it all and stood up and kept fighting . . . and won. the sheer amount of consistent work done through prolonged timeframe with exceptional discipline compounded so hard that it simply punched through the unlucky event.
